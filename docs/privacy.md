# Bloo - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

Bloo is a client for the Bluelink / Connected Car services of Hyundai, Genesis and Kia. It is an
independent app: it is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by** Hyundai Motor Company,
Genesis, Kia, or any of their subsidiaries.

This policy describes what the app does with your information. It is deliberately short, because the
app does very little with it.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored **on your device**, and the credentials are encrypted using the Android
Keystore:

- **Your Bluelink account email and password**, and your service PIN if you set one. These are the
  credentials you would otherwise type into the manufacturer's own app.
- **Your cars' status**: lock state, charge and fuel, climate, location, odometer, trips and service
  data, as fetched from the manufacturer's service.
- **Your app preferences**: theme, layout, alert settings, widget and watch options.

If you turn on **Drive sync**, your settings (not your password) are written to a file **in your own
Google Drive** using the account you pick, so your other devices can read them. That file is yours;
the app has no server and never sees it.

## Where your information is sent

- **To the manufacturer's own service** (Hyundai, Genesis or Kia, depending on your car), using your
  own account. This is the app's entire purpose: it is how your car is locked, started, located and
  read. That service's own privacy policy governs what it does with the request.
- **To GitHub**, a version check asking whether a newer build exists. It sends no account details,
  no car data and no identifier for you.
- **Nowhere else.** There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking, and
  no server operated by this app.

The **on-device AI summary** feature runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you summarise is sent
anywhere.

## What is never collected

- No name, address, phone number or contacts.
- No precise location of *you* - only of your car, and only because the manufacturer's service
  reports it.
- No data from other apps, and no data about how you use your phone.
- Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with data brokers or advertisers.

## Your control over it

- **Sign out** in Settings to delete your stored credentials.
- **Clear the app's data** in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it stored.
- If you enabled Drive sync, delete the sync file from your own Google Drive.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Permissions

The app asks only for what a given feature needs, and each is optional: location (to find your car
and to run AutoLock), notifications (car alerts), Bluetooth (AutoLock with your car's own
connection), and activity recognition (AutoLock's driving detection). The Play Store listing lists
them all.
