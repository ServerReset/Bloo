What it does

The things you would open the app for.

Your car's status, and the controls that change it, in one place.

Lock and unlock

Lock or unlock the doors, and see the lock state the car reports.

Climate

Start the climate with a preset, or let smart climate pick a temperature for the weather where the car is parked. Seat and wheel heat are included where the car has them.

Charging

The charge level, the range, the time left, and the limit you have set.

Location

Find the car on a map, with your phone's position beside it when you allow it.

AutoLock

Locks the car when you walk away from it.

Widgets and Wear OS

Lock, charge and climate on the home screen. A watch face with quick controls, tiles, and complications.

Several accounts

Sign in to more than one account and move between cars.

Tablets and foldables

Several cars side by side, with the cards in an order you choose.

How to get it

Three steps.

  1. Join the testing group

    Use the Google account on your phone. Join the Bloo testing group.

  2. Install from Google Play

    Open the Bloo page on Google Play, tap Become a tester, then install. Play keeps it up to date.

  3. Sign in

    Use your Bluelink, Genesis or Kia Connect account. Your cars appear once you are in.

Without Google Play, download the APK from the newest release. The Play copy and the APK copy are signed differently and cannot update each other, so switching between them means one uninstall first.

Your data

Your password stays on your phone.

Your Bluelink or Kia password and any service PIN are encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM. The key for that is kept in the Android Keystore, the hardware security chip in your phone.

  • They are sent only to your car maker's service, to sign in and to send commands.
  • Bloo runs no server that could keep them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service.
  • Summaries from the on-device AI run on the phone and are not sent anywhere.
  • If you turn on sync, your settings go to a file in your own Google Drive. Bloo never sees that file.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this the official app?

No. Bloo is independent. It uses your own account with the same services as the official app. It is not affiliated with Hyundai, Genesis or Kia.

Which cars work?

Hyundai, Genesis and Kia cars on Bluelink or Connected Car in the US, Canada and Europe. A feature only appears when your car supports it.

Does it work on an iPhone?

No. Bloo is for Android phones, tablets, foldables and Wear OS watches.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

Sign out in Settings to remove the stored credentials. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Bloo is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Hyundai Motor Company, Genesis, Kia, or any of their subsidiaries.
