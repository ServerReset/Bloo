Privacy

Your keys never leave your phone.

Credentials, service PINs, session tokens and the app-lock PIN live in one AES-256-GCM vault, sealed by the hardware-backed Android Keystore.

  • No server. The app talks to your manufacturer's service and nowhere else.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no crash-reporting SDK.
  • AI stays local. Summaries run on the phone with Gemini Nano.
  • Your Drive, your file. Sync, if you turn it on, writes to your own Google Drive.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this the official app?

No. Bloo is independent and not affiliated with Hyundai, Genesis or Kia. It uses your own account to talk to the same services the official app does.

Where do my password and PIN go?

They are stored on your device in an encrypted vault and sent only to the manufacturer's own service, to sign in and send commands. Nothing goes to a Bloo server, because there isn't one.

Which cars and regions work?

Hyundai, Genesis and Kia with Bluelink or Connected Car in the US and Canada, plus Hyundai Europe. A feature only shows if your car supports it.

Play or APK?

Same features. Play updates the app and installs the watch app for you. The APK updates itself (silently with Shizuku) and pushes the watch app over wireless debugging. They can't update each other.

Does the AI send my data anywhere?

No. Summaries and natural-language commands run on the phone with Gemini Nano.

What about iPhone?

Bloo is Android and Wear OS only.
