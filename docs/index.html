Features

Everything your car does, one tap away.

Built on Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive. Every control talks to the manufacturer's live service.

Lock and unlock

Lock, unlock, start climate and set charge limits. Lock state updates as the car reports it.

Charge at a glance

Level, range, time left, and the limit marked right on the bar.

Remote climate

Presets, seats and wheel heat. Smart climate targets a temperature for the weather where the car is parked.

Live location

Find the car on a map, with the phone's own dot beside it.

AutoLock

Locks the car when you walk away from it.

Widgets and Wear OS

Lock, charge and climate on the home screen, a watch face with quick controls, and tiles.

Built for foldables and tablets

Several cars side by side, each reflowing to two columns when there is room. Reorder the cards however you like.

Look

Light, dark, and your own colours.

Frosted glass over a living background. The palette can follow your wallpaper, and the card carries your car's photo when you add one.

Privacy

Your keys stay on your phone.

Your password and PIN are sealed in a vault on the phone, using the hardware-backed Android Keystore.

No Bloo server

The app talks to your manufacturer's service and nowhere else.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads and no crash-reporting SDK.

AI stays local

Summaries run on the phone with Gemini Nano. Nothing you summarise leaves it.

Your Drive, your file

If you turn on sync, settings go to a file in your own Google Drive.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this the official app?

No. Bloo is independent and isn't affiliated with Hyundai, Genesis or Kia. It signs in with your own account, the same way the official app does.

Where do my password and PIN go?

They're stored on your phone, in an encrypted vault, and only sent to the manufacturer's service to sign in and send commands.

Play or APK?

The same features. Play updates Bloo for you. The APK checks for updates itself. The two can't update each other.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Bloo is for Android and Wear OS.
